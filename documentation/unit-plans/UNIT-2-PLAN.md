### u2-induction: Induction through sigma bonds
- Stage / kind / role: A / spine / video
- Teaches: u2:induction
- Assumes: u0:resonance, u0:orbitals, u0:conjugate-base (NEW primer)
- Primer: big question "How can an atom change an acid's strength from two bonds away?" (kept);
  pretest `u2t-ind-distance`; ideas: electronegative atoms pull; passed along sigma bonds; fades
  with distance; adds up with number (kept, `content/u2tools/priming.ts`).
- Worked example: **NEW**. Rank butanoic acid `CCCC(=O)O`, 3-fluorobutanoic acid
  `CC(F)CC(=O)O` and 2-fluorobutanoic acid `CCC(F)C(=O)O` by acidity. Steps: (1) draw each
  conjugate base, because acidity is argued on the base; (2) the charge sits on the same CO2-
  in all three, so no resonance difference; (3) F two bonds from the carboxylate carbon pulls more
  than F three bonds away; none pulls least; (4) answer 2-F > 3-F > none. Figure: the three
  carboxylates, skeletal, F circled, bond count to the CO2- marked. Species not in the pool
  (pool uses chloroacetic acids and chloro positions on other chains; grep confirmed no
  fluorobutanoic acid). Order to be derived by `u2_tools_pools.py` as an induction rule, checked
  against a cited pKa table (claim: 2-F about 2.6, 3-F about 3.6, butanoic 4.8; **verify**).
- First step (fade line): "First step: draw the base each acid makes, then find the
  electronegative atom nearest its charge." (Holds for every item: all are acid or base ranks.)
- Practice (play order): u2t-ind-meet-why mcq L0, u2t-ind-meet-bond mcq L0, u2t-ind-distance

  u2t-ind-ladder sort L1 (*retag* from difficulty 1100 to L1), u2t-ind-halogen mcq L2,

- Why items: **NEW u2-ind-why-base** (L1, "why is ClCH2CO2H stronger: the pull steadies the

  the weaker acid; the gap says how far; check the acid formed (kept).

### u2-charged: Charged aromatic rings
- Stage / kind / role: B / spine / practice (*retagged* from video)
- Teaches: u2:charged-rings
- Assumes: u2:huckel, u2:keq-pka, u0:formal-charge (NEW primer)
- Primer: "Can a ring that carries a charge still be aromatic?" (kept); pretest
  `u2-chg-tropylium`; ideas: a lone pair joins the ring; empty orbital counts zero; ring class
  ranks stability; aromatic base, stronger acid (kept).
- Worked example: **NEW**. The cyclooctatetraene dianion `[CH-]1C=CC=CC=C[CH-]1` (RDKit: C8H8,
  charge −2, ring flagged aromatic). Steps: (1) neutral COT is a tub (Stage B pool); (2) add two
  electrons: each charged carbon keeps a lone pair in a p orbital; (3) count 6 from the three C=C
  plus 2 + 2 = 10, n = 2; (4) a 4n + 2 ring gains by lying flat, so the dianion is planar and
  aromatic. Figure: neutral tub and flat dianion, side by side (3-D model, 8e spatial row). Not in
  the pool. **Claim to derive**: planarity of the dianion is an authored call, as for every ring in
  `u2_pools.py`; RDKit only confirms the count.
- First step: "First step: decide what the charged carbon holds, a lone pair (counts 2) or an

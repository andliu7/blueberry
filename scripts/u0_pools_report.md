### 3. Electronegativity (table `EN`), for the delta plus carbon

Read from: OpenStax Organic Chemistry 2.1 (Polar Covalent Bonds and Electronegativity), text and Figure 2.3. Rule (OpenStax 2.1): in a bond from carbon to a more electronegative atom the electrons are drawn toward that atom, so carbon is delta plus.
